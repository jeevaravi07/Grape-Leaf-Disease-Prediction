About GrapeGuard AI

Bridging the gap between advanced artificial intelligence and everyday agriculture.

The Project

This application was built to assist farmers, viticulturists, and gardeners in identifying grape leaf diseases before they can ruin a harvest. By simply taking a photo of a suspicious leaf, users can bypass days of uncertainty and instantly know exactly what pathogen is attacking their crops—and more importantly, how to stop it.

The Technology
Deep Learning

Powered by a MobileNetV2 convolutional neural network, leveraging transfer learning to accurately analyze complex leaf textures and spot patterns.

Real-time Inference

Images are processed on the fly. The model mathematically calculates the probability of each disease to provide a high-confidence diagnosis in seconds.

Our Mission

Crop diseases threaten food security and farmer livelihoods worldwide. By making expert-level agricultural knowledge instantly accessible through a simple web interface, we aim to reduce pesticide misuse, prevent crop loss, and promote healthier, more sustainable vineyards.
