About GrapeGuard AI

Bridging the gap between advanced artificial intelligence and everyday agriculture.

Project Overview

GrapeGuard AI is an artificial intelligence-based agricultural support system developed to identify diseases affecting grape leaves through digital image processing and deep learning techniques. Grape cultivation plays an important role in agriculture, but grape plants are vulnerable to various diseases that can negatively affect plant growth, fruit quality, and overall crop productivity. Early identification of these diseases is essential for protecting crops and reducing agricultural losses.

This application provides a simple and efficient platform where farmers, agricultural researchers, and vineyard owners can upload grape leaf images for automated disease classification. The uploaded image is processed and analyzed using a trained MobileNetV2 deep learning model. Based on the learned visual patterns, the system predicts the most likely disease category and displays the corresponding prediction confidence.

By combining computer vision, image preprocessing, and deep learning, GrapeGuard AI aims to make preliminary grape leaf disease screening more accessible. It helps users obtain an initial indication of possible disease conditions without depending entirely on manual visual inspection. The prediction should be treated as decision-support information and not as a replacement for professional agricultural diagnosis.

The Technology
Deep Learning

Powered by a MobileNetV2 convolutional neural network that uses transfer learning to recognize disease-related visual patterns and distinguish between different grape leaf conditions.

Real-Time Inference

The uploaded leaf image is processed and passed through the trained model to generate a disease prediction and its associated confidence score.

Image Processing

Image preprocessing techniques such as resizing, normalization, and pixel-level preparation help maintain consistent input dimensions for the deep learning model.

Prediction Analysis

The system evaluates the model's output probabilities and presents the predicted disease category along with a confidence percentage for easier interpretation.

Our Mission

Crop diseases threaten food security and farmer livelihoods worldwide. Our mission is to make artificial intelligence-based agricultural screening accessible to farmers, researchers, and vineyard owners. By providing a convenient platform for preliminary grape leaf disease identification, GrapeGuard AI aims to encourage timely crop inspection, support informed agricultural decisions, minimize avoidable crop losses, and promote sustainable farming practices.

Our Vision

To develop an intelligent agricultural ecosystem where advanced AI and computer vision technologies help farmers monitor plant health, identify potential diseases, and make better crop management decisions.

Future Scope

Future enhancements may include mobile application support, additional grape disease categories, explainable AI, treatment recommendation support, multilingual interfaces, and integration with IoT-based agricultural monitoring systems.

Important Information

GrapeGuard AI provides AI-generated preliminary grape leaf disease predictions based on image classification. Results may be affected by image quality, lighting, leaf condition, and the training dataset. The prediction is not a confirmed laboratory diagnosis. Users should consult qualified agricultural experts or plant pathologists before making important crop treatment decisions.
